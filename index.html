Create your QR

With this Web App you can create your own QR codes without relying on third parties, without internet, private, secure and free.

What is private.qr?

It is a WebApp that seeks to offer users an empowering, private, offline service, without depending on third parties and free of charge.

Future features

  • Local history of created and read QRs
  • QR Reader
  • Custom QR
  • Link shortener
  • Temporary QR
